# Product Vision

## Vision

RetroBoy should make returning to a Game Boy or Game Boy Color game on a Mac feel as immediate and considered as opening a favorite album: a calm library, a trustworthy play surface, and powerful preservation tools that stay out of the way.

## Audience

The primary audience is a macOS user on Apple Silicon who owns or is otherwise legally entitled to use their game dumps and values polish, local ownership, and predictable behavior over feature volume.

## Product promise

- Start and resume games with minimal friction.
- Preserve progress reliably and make every destructive action explicit or recoverable.
- Offer modern conveniences—rewind, cheats, shaders, and bezels—without compromising a coherent experience.
- Keep games, saves, preferences, and play history private and local.
- Feel at home on macOS while retaining a visual identity of its own.

## Principles

1. **Trust before novelty.** Save integrity, frame pacing, audio stability, and clear data ownership come first.
2. **The game is the hero.** Chrome supports discovery and control, then recedes during play.
3. **Depth on demand.** Common actions are obvious; advanced controls are discoverable without crowding the default surface.
4. **Local by default.** No account, telemetry, or network dependency is required.
5. **Faithful, not nostalgic by accident.** Retro references must serve comprehension or delight, not imitate hardware limitations.

## Success signals for V1

- A user can import, organize, launch, play, save, resume, rewind, and customize a supported game without external documentation.
- Save and rewind operations survive interruption and failure tests without silent data loss.
- Representative GB/GBC workloads meet agreed audio, frame-pacing, latency, and energy targets on the minimum supported Apple Silicon Mac.
- Core flows work with keyboard, common controllers, VoiceOver-relevant semantics, reduced motion, and high-contrast needs.

## Non-goals for V1

Cross-platform releases, additional consoles, multiplayer, cloud sync, accounts, achievements, a ROM store, and bundled copyrighted game content are not V1 goals.
