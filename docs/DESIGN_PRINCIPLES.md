# Design Principles

## 1. Put the game first

The player surface is the visual center. Controls appear when needed, communicate state, and recede without becoming hard to recover.

## 2. Make progress feel safe

Saves, states, rewind, imports, and deletions must reveal what will happen. Prefer previews, undo, recovery copies, and non-destructive exploration over confirmation dialogs alone.

## 3. Use nostalgia with restraint

Pixel references, hardware cues, sound, and motion should reinforce identity or comprehension. Do not mimic low-resolution typography, cramped controls, or inaccessible hardware constraints in application chrome.

## 4. Feel native without becoming anonymous

Honor macOS expectations for windows, menus, shortcuts, focus, full screen, drag/drop, and system dialogs. Build a distinct RetroBoy character through color, typography, composition, and tactile details—not by breaking platform behavior.

## 5. Reveal depth progressively

Launching and resuming a game should be simple. Cheats, shader parameters, bezel management, state metadata, and diagnostics can live in well-organized secondary surfaces.

## 6. Design every state

Blank libraries, missing files, invalid ROMs, controller disconnects, incompatible states, shader failures, storage errors, and interrupted writes are product states, not afterthoughts.

## 7. Be fast in perception and fact

Immediate feedback, stable layout, predictable transitions, and uninterrupted input matter alongside measured startup, latency, and frame pacing. Decorative motion must never delay control.

## 8. Support different ways of playing

Keyboard, controller, pointer, and assistive-technology paths should coexist. Do not make hover, drag, color, sound, or fine motor precision the only route to an action.

## 9. Keep customization reversible

Shaders, bezels, covers, controls, cheats, and metadata need previews, defaults, reset paths, and safe failure behavior.

## 10. Prefer coherence over collection

External UI examples are references, not a shopping list. New patterns must earn their place and be adapted to RetroBoy tokens, behavior, language, and accessibility.
